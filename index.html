What is Proof of Humanity (PoH) and why does it matter?

Proof of Humanity (PoH) is a decentralized identity verification system that ensures users are real humans and not bots. It combines social verification with biometric data to create a unique digital identity on the blockchain.
In the context of Node_Zero, PoH is important because it allows users to access certain features, such as minting Lockb0x Sigil NFTs for free and participating in the Exponent Participation Rewards Pool.

To get PoH, follow the verification process at the Linea.Build Hub.

Not interested in PoH? You can still mint a Lockb0x Sigil NFT by paying the required amount, but you won't be eligible for the rewards pool.
